01

Research

Five threads, from detector physics to deployed tools. Click any to open.

  • Built an end-to-end reconstruction pipeline for an Insight46 cohort subset ([18F]florbetapir PET/MR, Siemens Biograph mMR), working from raw list-mode detector events with NiftyPET — over 12,000 GPU reconstructions on UCL's HPC cluster.
  • Designed an uncertainty-quantification study of short-frame imaging, combining temporal subsampling with Poisson bootstrap resampling of list-mode events across ~11,000 independent image realisations, separating statistical noise from real signal change.
  • Fine-tuned a cross-tracer deep-learning denoising model and evaluated it on held-out subjects against an empirically measured noise floor rather than an arbitrary baseline.
  • Regional quantification with GIF parcellation in native PET space, validated against an independent reconstruction of the same cohort — isolating the separate contributions of acquisition window, attenuation map and processing.
  • Engineered the infrastructure: resumable HPC array jobs with GPU pre-flight checks, automated QC, interactive reporting dashboards, and reproducible notebooks — including fixing faults found in the reconstruction toolchain itself.

  • MRIQC is the field standard for automated MRI quality assessment and it assumes an HPC cluster. Most African sites have neither the cluster nor the bandwidth. WebMRIQC removes that dependency entirely.
  • Browser-based implementation with DICOM-to-BIDS conversion built in; automated extraction of SNR, CNR, FD, DVARS and related metrics; 5–10 min per T1 volume over a standard internet connection.
  • Adopted by the SWiM Program 2026. Accepted to MICCAI MIRASOL 2026; this was my undergraduate final thesis.

Brain-AID

My institution had no neuroimaging analysis training and no research programme at all. I started Brain-AID in my penultimate undergraduate year to pass on the training, software skills and conference exposure I had gained through international collaborations, to students who had no other route to them. It now has 350+ members across 74 institutions in 5 countries, with members presenting at national and international conferences, and is formally recognised by the College of Medicine.
